# Quick Start Guide - Phone Repair Simulator

## 🚀 Getting Started in 30 Seconds

### 1. Open the Game
Open `phone-repair-simulator.html` in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge recommended).

### 2. First Screen - Workshop View
You'll see a professional phone repair shop from above. 
- **Top bar** shows your money ($500 start), level, reputation, and phones repaired
- **Computer on desk** (light blue box with 💻) - Click this to access jobs

### 3. Access the Computer
Click on the computer in the workshop to open the **Phone Repair Shop OS**.

### 4. Accept Your First Job
1. The "Jobs" tab opens by default
2. You'll see 6 available repair jobs
3. Each job card shows:
   - Phone model (e.g., "Apple iPhone 14")
   - Problem type (e.g., "Cracked screen")
   - Difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard)
   - Payment amount ($$$)
4. Click **ACCEPT** on any job to start repairs

### 5. Repair the Phone
You're now at the repair workstation. You'll see:
- **Phone details** - What phone and what problem
- **Action buttons** - Diagnostics, Repair, Test, Complete
- **Repair steps** - A checklist of repair procedures
- **Repair log** - Messages during repair

### 6. Complete Steps
1. Click **🔍 Diagnostics** to examine the phone
   - Complete all four diagnostic tests to unlock repairs
2. Click **🔧 Repair** to see step-by-step instructions
   - Close the instructions and grab tools directly from the mat by pressing and dragging
   - Circle each bottom screw counterclockwise to loosen it, clockwise to tighten it
   - Follow each circular guide for one full turn. Both screws must turn green;
     the next step starts automatically after the second screw is finished.
   - Trace the phone edges with the heat gun and opening pick
   - The opening pick automatically opens the phone at 100% seam coverage;
     next, use tweezers to disconnect the battery
   - With the heat gun, hold near an edge until coverage and warmth both reach
     100%; the game advances automatically, then use the suction cup to lift
   - Follow grab points and arrows to lift the screen, disconnect cables, and move parts
   - Each fault type has its own procedure: battery swaps (pull the adhesive tab),
     cleaning jobs (scrub inside the dashed area with the IPA brush), board repairs
     (lift the EMI shield, then probe the red pad before the black pad with the
     multimeter), water damage, back-glass replacement, and screen re-adhesion
   - Drag the spare part into its bay, reconnect the cable, replace the cover, and tighten the screws
   - Clicking a tool or the phone alone will not complete a step
3. Click **✓ Test** to verify your fix; testing is required before payment
4. Click **✅ Complete** when done
   - You get paid immediately!
   - Money, reputation, and level increase

To take a break, choose **Pause & return**. In the computer's **Active Repairs**
tab, click the paused job's card or **Continue job**. Your saved phone and partial
repair progress remain available after reloading the game.
Completed and rejected offers are replaced by new jobs and remain off the board
after a reload. The board mixes repair types and labels each service, including
cleaning. Offers can return once the job catalog has been exhausted.

### 7. Upgrade Your Business
Click the computer again and go to **⬆️ Upgrades** tab:
- Buy better tools, workspace, faster computer, etc.
- Each upgrade costs money but improves your business
- Better equipment = faster repairs = more money

### 8. Monitor Progress
Check the **📈 Statistics** tab to see:
- Total money earned
- Phones repaired
- Your level and reputation
- Completed jobs

### 9. Save Your Game
- Auto-saves every 30 seconds
- Manually click **💾 Save Game** button anytime
- Game saves to your browser (offline)

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Game Won't Load
- Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Ensure JavaScript is enabled

### Buttons Not Working
- Wait for current action to complete
- Try clicking again
- Refresh page if stuck

### Game Won't Save
- Check if localStorage is enabled
- Try manual save button
- Restart browser
- Check available storage space

### Performance Issues
- Close other browser tabs
- Disable browser extensions
- Update your browser
- Try different browser
